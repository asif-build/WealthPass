● Claimant Rights under RBI Directions (RBI/2025-26/82)

15 Calendar Days Timeline: Banks are legally bound to settle deceased depositor claims within a maximum of 15 calendar days from receiving the complete claim package.
Delay Compensation: If settlement is delayed beyond 15 days without statutory cause, the bank is liable to pay penal compensation at the applicable rate.

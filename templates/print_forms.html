Statutory Banking Claim Protocol (Bank Form 14)

Under Reserve Bank of India Master Circulars on Settlement of Claims in respect of Deceased Depositors:

  1. Branch Jurisdiction: Submit this consolidated packet along with Bank Form 14 (Deceased Depositor Claim Application) to the servicing branch of the holding bank.
  2. Balance Disclosure: Official portals (such as RBI UDGAM) confirm the existence of an unclaimed deposit without publishing private balances on the public internet. The holding branch calculates interest and discloses final balance upon verification of legal heirship.
  3. Indemnity & NOC: In non-nominee claims up to the bank's threshold limit, claims are settled on the basis of a stamped Letter of Indemnity and NOC from other legal heirs without requiring a court Succession Certificate.
  4. Disbursement: Settlement is credited directly to the claimant's verified bank account via NEFT/RTGS.
